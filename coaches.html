How the We Know the W Rating is calculated

The We Know the W Rating is a 0–100 editorial score that balances career results, 2026 performance, Finals history and documented program impact.

  • Career regular-season win percentage through 2025 × 10
  • 2026 regular-season win percentage × 15
  • Career playoff win percentage through 2025 × 10
  • 2026 playoff finish: champion 35, Finals participant 30, semifinal exit 22, first-round exit 14, missed playoffs 0
  • 2.5 points per WNBA title as head coach, capped at 10; prior Finals appearances add up to 5
  • Time to first WNBA Finals: 10 points in seasons 1–2, 8 in seasons 3–4, 6 in seasons 5–6, 4 in seasons 7–10, 2 after season 10; no Finals trip earns 0
  • Program impact adds up to 5: up to 2.5 for the team’s 2025–26 win-rate improvement and up to 2.5 for documented culture or franchise milestones

For a first WNBA head-coaching season, career points move into the current-season components: up to 25 for the regular season and 45 for the playoff finish. Finals pace counts WNBA head-coaching seasons, including the current season. Program impact compares full team records and is an editorial signal, not proof of a single coach’s causal effect; its evidence appears in each coach spotlight. Expansion teams without a 2025 record receive no year-over-year trend points. 2026 Finals participants are scored for reaching the Finals, while 2026 title points wait until the series is complete. Coach of the Year honors remain visible as context. Totals round to the nearest whole point; this is not an official WNBA statistic.
