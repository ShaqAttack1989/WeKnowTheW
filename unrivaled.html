PLAYER-COACH DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM

Brittney Griner is competing and learning to coach.

The founding Unrivaled player will pilot the league’s new Player Coach Development Program. With mentorship from Unrivaled head coaches and hands-on coaching experience, Griner will build leadership and coaching skills while staying on the court.

Griner played for Phantom BC in 2025 and Vinyl BC in 2026, averaging 13.0 points and 5.0 rebounds per game across her Unrivaled career.

UNRIVALED 101 + HISTORY

A league built for the offseason.

Founded by Napheesa Collier and Breanna Stewart, Unrivaled created a domestic 3 on 3 option with player equity, premium production and a Miami home base.

2024LEAGUE ANNOUNCED

Unrivaled is introduced as a new player-centered 3 on 3 professional league.

2025SEASON ONE

Six clubs debut. Lunar Owls finish 13–1, Rose wins the first championship, Napheesa Collier wins MVP and the first one on one tournament.

2025FIRST EXPANSION

Breeze BC and Hive BC are announced, taking the league from six clubs to eight for Season 2.

2026SEASON TWO

Phantom wins the regular season, Mist wins the championship, and Chelsea Gray wins MVP and the one on one crown.

2027SEASON THREE

All 48 players are confirmed, and Season 3 will tour eight cities from January through March while keeping Miami as its home base.
