THE WE KNOW THE W READ

Do not call this the junior varsity.

The jerseys say senior national team and the games count. The opportunity is developmental, but the assignment is a championship.

THE BREAKOUT

Mikayla Blakes owned the biggest game.

Blakes scored 27 in the final and won tournament MVP. That is the AmeriCup’s value in one line: a young player can move from prospect conversation to senior national team proof in a week.

THE CONNECTOR

Olivia Miles controlled the final.

Miles delivered nine assists against Brazil. Her creation helped USA turn the fourth quarter, showing how a player’s national team role can reveal a different layer than her club assignment.

THE PAYOFF

Gold became a ticket to Berlin.

The 2025 title directly qualified the United States for the 2026 World Cup. The path from continental championship to global stage is exactly why this event belongs in the Team USA hierarchy.
