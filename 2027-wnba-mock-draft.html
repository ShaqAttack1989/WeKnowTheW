EDITION 01 · SEP. 3

SHAK’S TAKE

The top is about certainty. The rest is about fit.

This is not a final board. It is the first scouting argument before the 2026-27 season supplies new evidence.

Why Houston and JuJu make sense

Houston is not walking into 2027 as a normal expansion team. It is the relocated Connecticut franchise, which means the Comets inherit the roster, contracts and draft assets attached to the Sun. They also inherit the league’s weakest current two-season record. That is why this board begins in Houston.

Watkins is the player who changes the scale of the rebuild. She has already shown the hard part: creating quality offense when everybody in the building knows she has to create it. At 6'2", she can see and shoot over smaller guards, punish switches, draw fouls and produce defensive events away from the ball. The health check matters after her ACL injury, but the upside remains No. 1.

The first argument starts at No. 2

Hannah Hidalgo owns the loudest two-way résumé. Her pressure defense can change possession after possession, and 25.3 points with 5.2 assists shows she is much more than a disruptor. Madison Booker offers a different answer: a 6'1" wing creator who can score from the middle of the floor and organize offense when the first action breaks.

S’Mya Nichols, MiLaysia Fulwiley and Mikaylah Williams make the next tier guard-heavy. Nichols brings size and playmaking, Fulwiley brings burst and defensive chaos, and Williams brings polished shot creation. Clara Strack is the first interior anchor on this board because 6'5" size, double-digit rebounding and 2.5 blocks travel.

What could blow up this board

The lottery is the obvious answer, but it is not the only one. Watkins has to return healthy. Prospects have to enter. New roles and transfers can change usage. International players can rise quickly once front offices get complete medicals and live evaluations. And team fit will look different after another offseason of trades and free agency.

So save the screenshot, not the certainty. The value of an early mock is seeing the assumptions before the games start changing them.
