ONE WORD, DIFFERENT MEANINGS

What counts as expansion here?

THE WNew franchises

New owners, cities, arenas, identities and permanent rosters.

UPSHOTNew markets

More development clubs create additional professional roster opportunities.

UNRIVALEDNew clubs + larger pool

Expansion adds both teams and more offseason jobs for elite players.

ATHLETES UNLIMITEDStructural evolution

No permanent franchises. Growth is better measured through roster depth, branding, format and continuity.

Expansion Watch rule

Confirmed facts are treated as confirmed. Announced-but-unfinished pieces stay labeled pending. A future league change does not become “official” because it would make a prettier card.
