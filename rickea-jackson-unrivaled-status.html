The headline “returning to Unrivaled” is true in the big picture. It is not the same thing as saying Rickea Jackson will play in Season 3.

The announcement needs the injury update beside it

Unrivaled’s current status message says Jackson is part of the league on a multi year deal, but she will miss the 2027 season because of injury. The league says she is expected to return in Season 4.

That distinction matters. A return graphic can describe contractual commitment while still leaving a playing season unavailable. We Know the W is carrying both facts together so the Playerpedia and No Offseason record does not turn a contract announcement into a false availability report.

Why Unrivaled would want the relationship to continue

Jackson has already produced in the format. Unrivaled’s official player bio lists career bests of 14.9 points, 6.0 rebounds and 2.8 assists across 12 games for Breeze in 2026. She also hit her first career game winner in Breeze’s debut and recorded a season high 22 points against Vinyl.

Her league history already spans Mist in 2025 and Breeze in 2026. The multi year deal extends that relationship even though the next playing chapter has to wait.
