THE PIPELINE

AU is an offseason bridge, not a one-way feeder.

Veteran WNBA players, free agents and younger pros share the same pool, then carry those reps back into WNBA camps, overseas seasons and other domestic leagues.

WNBA VETERANSStay game-ready

Established players use AU to compete through the winter without leaving the United States.

FREE AGENTSFresh film, fresh evaluation

AU creates current professional tape and measurable performance before WNBA camp decisions.

EMERGING PROSShare the floor with veterans

Younger players get reps against WNBA-level experience instead of waiting for a single tryout window.

PLAYERPEDIA LINKStatus travels with the player

HOW AU WORKS

Fantasy basketball logic, actual professional stakes.

01 · ONE PLAYER POOL40 active pros

The full field lives in one league roster rather than four permanent franchise rosters.

02 · CAPTAINSTop four every week

After each week, the four highest leaderboard scorers become captains for the next draft.

03 · WEEKLY DRAFTEverybody gets reshuffled

Captains draft Gold Rush, Glow, Rhythm and Eclipse from the available player pool.

04 · INDIVIDUAL POINTSWins + stats + MVP votes

The leaderboard follows the player even when her teammates and team identity change.

05 · FINAL CROWNNo playoff bracket needed

When the final game ends, the player with the most leaderboard points is the season champion.

FIVE SEASONS OF EXPERIMENTING

From Team Sims to Gold Rush.

AU kept the core weekly-draft format while changing cities, presentation and team identity over time.

2022INAUGURAL SEASON

The league debuts with weekly captain-named teams and Tianna Hawkins becomes the first basketball champion.

2023SEASON TWO

NaLyssa Smith claims the individual championship.

2024SEASON THREE

Allisha Gray wins the crown as the player-first scoring model becomes familiar to fans.

2025NASHVILLE ERA

Maddy Siegrist wins Season 4. Teams are still identified by captains in the final week, such as Team Siegrist and Team Sims.

2026NAMED TEAM IDENTITIES

Gold Rush, Glow, Rhythm and Eclipse replace the old color-only/captain-facing identity while weekly redrafts remain intact. Odyssey Sims wins Season 5.

2027PAUSE

AU announces no basketball season while it evaluates the league’s future structure and long-term impact.
