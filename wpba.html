LEAGUE PULSE

A summer league built to move careers forward.

The WPBA is a FIBA-sanctioned professional development league based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Graduating seniors, former college players, national team athletes, and current or former professionals compete in an eight-team season from June through August.

THE PATHWAY

Development is the product.

The WPBA says more than 100 athletes have advanced to higher levels in its first four seasons. That includes opportunities in the WNBA, Athletes Unlimited, and professional leagues around the world.

01

Get professional film

A compensated summer season gives players current game tape, statistics and role clarity against professional competition.

02

Stay on the radar

The league creates another evaluation window for graduating seniors, free agents, national team players and pros between jobs.

03

Reach the next league

The goal is movement, including WNBA training camps, domestic opportunities, national teams, and contracts overseas.
