How Career Legacy and 2026 Form are calculated

Both scores use a fixed 0–100 editorial scale; neither is a percentile or rank against the current coach pool.

  • Career Legacy (through 2025): career regular-season win percentage × 10; career playoff win percentage × 10; 13⅓ points per WNBA title as head coach, capped at 40; 4 points per Finals appearance, capped at 20; 3 points per Coach of the Year award, capped at 10; and up to 10 for reaching a first Finals quickly.
  • 2026 Form: 2026 regular-season win percentage × 30; playoff finish worth 40 for champion, 35 for a Finals appearance, 25 for a semifinal exit, 15 for a quarterfinal exit, or 0 if the team missed the playoffs; up to 10 for team win-rate improvement, 10 for documented program or culture impact, 5 for 2026 Coach of the Year, and 5 for a first-ever Finals appearance in 2026.

Finals pace awards 10 points for a first trip in seasons 1–2, 8 in seasons 3–4, 6 in seasons 5–6, 4 in seasons 7–10 and 2 after season 10. Career Legacy excludes 2026 results; they appear only in 2026 Form. Team improvement compares full 2025 and 2026 team records; expansion teams without a 2025 record receive no trend points. Program and culture points are an editorial signal, with evidence shown in each spotlight. These scores describe different time frames, not the full historical WNBA coach pool, and are not official WNBA statistics.
