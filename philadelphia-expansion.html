NAME + LOGO + IDENTITY WATCH

Philly has to feel like Philly.

These are identity questions, not official branding predictions. The challenge is to create something rooted in Philadelphia basketball culture without turning the WNBA team into a smaller copy of the 76ers.

ATTITUDELoud, proud, local

The eventual brand should feel comfortable in a city where sports identity is neighborhood-level personal.

HISTORYPast without costume

Philadelphia's colonial and civic symbols are obvious tools, but the strongest identity may reinterpret them rather than simply reuse them.

BASKETBALLStreet-court credibility

Philly's basketball language comes from gyms, playgrounds, high schools, colleges and pro history — a deeper well than just red, white and blue.

WHAT IS CONFIRMED

The infrastructure is arriving before the logo.

2025

Philadelphia awarded a WNBA franchise

The league announced the market with a 2030 inaugural season and HBSE as the owner/operator.

2026

Formal approval

The WNBA and NBA Boards of Governors formally approved Philadelphia alongside Cleveland and Detroit.

NOW

Name and identity still pending

The team says the naming process will include Philadelphia fan and basketball-community input. Merchandise and full team branding follow later.

2030

Opening season

Philadelphia becomes the league's 18th team and the city's first WNBA franchise.
