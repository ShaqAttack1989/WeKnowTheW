THE EXPANSION CLUBS

Breeze + Hive entered differently.

Both were new brands, but their rosters pulled together established Unrivaled players, WNBA stars and newcomers to the league.

2026 roster included Cameron Brink, Paige Bueckers, Rickea Jackson, Dominique Malonga, Kate Martin, Aari McDonald and Courtney Williams. Brink, Jackson, Martin and Williams all had Year One Unrivaled connections before landing on the expansion club.

YEAR ONE → YEAR TWO

The original clubs did not simply run it back.

Expansion redistributed familiar names across the league. These snapshots show the cleanest club-to-club changes between the inaugural season and Season 2.

OWLSLUNAR OWLS

Napheesa Collier and Skylar Diggins stayed. Allisha Gray moved to Mist, Shakira Austin to Rose, and Courtney Williams and Cameron Brink landed with expansion Breeze. Aaliyah Edwards and Marina Mabrey joined the 2026 Owls.

MISTMIST BC

Breanna Stewart remained the anchor. Rickea Jackson moved to Breeze and Aaliyah Edwards to Lunar Owls. Allisha Gray and Arike Ogunbowale joined a reworked 2026 champion roster.

LACESLACES BC

Alyssa Thomas and Jackie Young remained. Kate Martin moved to Breeze, Tiffany Hayes to Phantom, while Brittney Sykes, Naz Hillmon, Jordin Canada and Maddy Siegrist joined the 2026 club.

ROSEROSE BC

Chelsea Gray, Angel Reese, Kahleah Copper and Lexie Hull stayed together. Azurá Stevens moved to Hive, Brittney Sykes to Laces, and Shakira Austin joined from Lunar Owls.

PHANPHANTOM BC

Natasha Cloud and Satou Sabally remained. Brittney Griner shifted to Vinyl and Marina Mabrey to Lunar Owls. Aliyah Boston, Kelsey Plum, Kiki Iriafen and others reshaped the 2026 roster.

VINYLVINYL BC

Rhyne Howard, Dearica Hamby and Rae Burrell stayed in the core. Arike Ogunbowale moved to Mist, Jordin Canada to Laces and Aliyah Boston to Phantom, while Brittney Griner arrived from Phantom.

Roster note

Year One clubs also used relief players as injuries changed availability. This comparison emphasizes the core season rosters and permanent Year Two assignments rather than every short-term replacement appearance.
