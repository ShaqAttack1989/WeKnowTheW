UNRIVALED 101 + HISTORY

A league built for the offseason.

Founded by Napheesa Collier and Breanna Stewart, Unrivaled created a domestic 3 on 3 option with player equity, premium production and a Miami home base.

2024LEAGUE ANNOUNCED

Unrivaled is introduced as a new player-centered 3 on 3 professional league.

2025SEASON ONE

Six clubs debut. Lunar Owls finish 13–1, Rose wins the first championship, Napheesa Collier wins MVP and the first one on one tournament.

2025FIRST EXPANSION

Breeze BC and Hive BC are announced, taking the league from six clubs to eight for Season 2.

2026SEASON TWO

Phantom wins the regular season, Mist wins the championship, and Chelsea Gray wins MVP and the one on one crown.

2027SEASON THREE

A new group of signees is already under contract. Club assignments and any additional structural changes remain on watch.
