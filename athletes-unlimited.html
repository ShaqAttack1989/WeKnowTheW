HOW AU WORKS

Fantasy basketball logic, actual professional stakes.

01 · ONE PLAYER POOL40 active pros

The full field lives in one league roster rather than four permanent franchise rosters.

02 · CAPTAINSTop four every week

After each week, the four highest leaderboard scorers become captains for the next draft.

03 · WEEKLY DRAFTEverybody gets reshuffled

Captains draft Gold Rush, Glow, Rhythm and Eclipse from the available player pool.

04 · INDIVIDUAL POINTSWins + stats + MVP votes

The leaderboard follows the player even when her teammates and team identity change.

05 · FINAL CROWNNo playoff bracket needed

When the final game ends, the player with the most leaderboard points is the season champion.
