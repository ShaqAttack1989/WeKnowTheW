GROWTH WITHOUT FRANCHISES

AU’s basketball model does not expand like the WNBA or UPSHOT. Its story is structural: deeper player pools, weekly redrafts, permanent team identities in 2026 and a confirmed 2027 pause while the league evaluates what comes next.

JULY 31, 2026

A pause is part of the history too.

Athletes Unlimited officially announced that Pro Basketball will pause during the 2027 season while the organization evaluates how the property can make its greatest long-term impact.

CONFIRMEDNo 2027 AU basketball season

The site will not manufacture a schedule, roster or champion for a year AU has explicitly paused.
