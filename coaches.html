How the WKTW career and 2026 grades are calculated

Both grades use a 55–99 peer-percentile scale, like Playerpedia. The comparison pool is the 15 current/recent coaches in this directory; it is not a full all-time WNBA coach ranking.

  • Career W Grade (through 2025): peer percentiles for WNBA regular-season win rate (45%), playoff win rate (25%, when the coach has at least eight playoff games), title conversion among Finals trips (20%), and time to a first Finals (10%). We reweight only the metrics with enough evidence. Fewer than 88 prior WNBA regular-season games or fewer than eight playoff games is marked provisional; a coach with no prior WNBA season is NR instead of receiving a zero. Titles, Finals trips, Coach of the Year awards, and total wins remain visible as résumé facts rather than points for longevity.
  • 2026 Form: peer percentiles for regular-season win rate (35%), playoff stage (30%), team win-rate change (15%), documented culture/program impact (10%), 2026 Coach of the Year (5%), and a first-ever Finals trip in 2026 (5%). If a culture-impact source has not been coded for a coach, that component is omitted and the remaining weights are rebalanced.

Percentiles use mid-ranks for ties and convert to 55–99. Missing or ineligible career data is not treated as poor performance. The Career W Grade is WNBA-only; college and other professional coaching records are not mixed into WNBA win rates. Current-season impact and turnaround appear in 2026 Form, while historical titles and awards remain beside the grade. These are editorial comparisons, not official WNBA statistics or a full historical coach pool ranking.
