We Know the W composite

Season score = 40 baseline points + 30 × regular-season win percentage + 20 × playoff win percentage + 8 for a championship + 2 for Coach of the Year. No playoff appearances earn zero playoff points. Scores are rounded to one decimal and sorted within each season.

This is an independent Unrivaled results composite, not a league award or a prediction. It rewards postseason delivery and titles without importing NBA, WNBA, college or playing achievements. It does not claim to measure culture or development without comparable evidence. A+, A, A−, B+, B, B−, C+, C and D begin at 90, 85, 80, 75, 70, 65, 60, 55 and below 55.

Unplayed seasons are not scored. For current coaches with completed league seasons, use the season selector to see their earlier scores.
