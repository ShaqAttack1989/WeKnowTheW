THE 30-SECOND BRIEFING

No passport required. An alarm clock helps.

Berlin is six hours ahead of Eastern time. The tournament runs September 4 through 13, expands to 16 teams for the first time, and squeezes 36 games into 10 days. Group winners earn a direct trip to the quarterfinals, which makes every early result matter.

WHENSept. 4–13

Group play ends Sept. 7. Qualification games are Sept. 8–9, quarterfinals Sept. 10, semifinals Sept. 12, medals Sept. 13.

WHEREBerlin

Games are staged at Berlin Arena and Max-Schmeling-Halle in Germany.

FIELD16 teams

Four groups of four, expanded from the 12-team field used in 2022.

THE PRIZEOne less game

Finish first in your group and skip the single-elimination qualification round.

Why the group winner matters: second and third place do advance, but they have to survive an extra elimination game. In a compressed tournament, winning the group buys the rarest luxury in Berlin: rest.

THE GROUPS

Four neighborhoods. Four ways to get in trouble.

The names are familiar, but the pressure is different. One loss can turn a clean quarterfinal route into an extra win-or-go-home game.

GROUP A

Spain · Germany · Japan · Mali

🇪🇸 Spain🇩🇪 Germany🇯🇵 Japan🇲🇱 Mali

The read: Spain enters on FIBA's latest Smart Power Rankings podium line, while Germany gets the home crowd and a frontcourt full of WNBA familiarity. Japan brings pace and Mai Yamamoto's larger national-team role. Mali has Sika Koné and no reason to play scared.

GROUP B

France · Hungary · Nigeria · Korea

🇫🇷 France🇭🇺 Hungary🇳🇬 Nigeria🇰🇷 Korea

The read: France is the biggest threat to USA on paper and owns the deepest WNBA contingent outside the Americans. Hungary turns Dorka Juhász into a featured star, Nigeria brings AfroBasket pedigree, and Korea can punish teams that lose shooters.

GROUP C

Belgium · Australia · Türkiye · Puerto Rico

🇧🇪 Belgium🇦🇺 Australia🇹🇷 Türkiye🇵🇷 Puerto Rico

The read: This is the group to circle in ink. Belgium's movement and Emma Meesseman meet Australia's size, experience and Sandy Brondello. Türkiye has Kennedy Burke, while Puerto Rico has the freedom of a team nobody should overlook.

GROUP D

USA · China · Italy · Czechia

🇺🇸 USA🇨🇳 China🇮🇹 Italy🇨🇿 Czechia

The read: USA is the favorite, but China gives the group serious interior size and Italy arrives with EuroBasket continuity plus Cecilia Zandalasini. Czechia's job is to make the favorites uncomfortable enough for one result to rearrange the bracket.

THE CLUB STORY

New York sends the biggest suitcase.

The WNBA primer counted seven Liberty players headed to Berlin, representing six countries. Minnesota followed with six, Portland with five, and Atlanta, Golden State, Las Vegas and Seattle with four apiece.

Chicago is the only WNBA team without a player in that primer, but Sky assistant Rena Wakama is still in Berlin as Nigeria's head coach.

SAME PLAYER · DIFFERENT JOB

National-team basketball changes the menu.

A WNBA fifth option can become the whole offense. A 34-minute WNBA star can become a 20-minute specialist. That role change is one of the best reasons to watch FIBA.

🇭🇺 DORKA JUHÁSZ

From Lynx role player to Hungary centerpiece

She averaged 18.0 points and 9.0 rebounds in qualifying, opened with 35 points, and made the All-Star Five while helping Hungary reach its first World Cup in 28 years.

🇯🇵 MAI YAMAMOTO

Bench spark in Vegas, engine for Japan

Yamamoto led Japan in qualifying scoring, threes and steals, then earned All-Star Five honors. Watch how often the ball is in her hands compared with her Aces role.

🇫🇷 JANELLE SALAÜN

Golden State weapon, France tournament MVP

Salaün led France's qualifying team in scoring, rebounding and made threes, earning qualifying tournament MVP honors with outrageous efficiency.

🇺🇸 RHYNE HOWARD

WNBA workhorse, USA luxury piece

Howard leads the WNBA in minutes per game, but Team USA can spread creation and defense across 12 elite players. Less usage does not mean less importance.

TEAM USA · DRIVE FOR FIVE

New generation, same gold standard.

USA is chasing a fifth consecutive World Cup title. A'ja Wilson and Kelsey Plum are out for health reasons, and Sonia Citron plus Kiki Iriafen have joined the final 12. Only five players from the 2024 Olympic team return, which makes this roster both overwhelmingly talented and unusually new.

WATCH LIKE IT'S FIBA

One rule wrinkle you will notice immediately.

This World Cup is still played under the 2024 FIBA rules. The new 2026 rule changes do not take effect until October 1, after the tournament.

RIM

Once the ball hits the rim, it is live.

Under FIBA basket-interference rules, players can tip or knock the ball after it touches the rim in situations that would still be protected by the imaginary cylinder in WNBA/NBA play. If a rebound suddenly looks like somebody swatted a ball off the iron, your eyes are not lying to you.
