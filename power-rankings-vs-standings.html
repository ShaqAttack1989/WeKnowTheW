OFFICIAL NO. 1Minnesota Lynx

The Lynx own the league's best record at 31 and 9.

POWER NO. 1Golden State Valkyries

A four win week, including a 14 point win over Minnesota, earns the apron.

BIGGEST SPLITMinnesota, down three

The Lynx sit first officially and fourth in the current power ranking.

Standings are the résumé. Power rankings are the temperature check.

Through games played August 30, Minnesota sits first at 31 and 9, Golden State follows at 29 and 11, and Las Vegas holds third at 27 and 13. That is the full season table. The latest We Know the W ranking asks a slightly different question: who is cooking right now?

Golden State took the top spot

The Valkyries went 4 and 0 during the ranking week, beat Minnesota by 14, held New York to 60 points and closed the week on a four game winning streak. That pushes Golden State one place above its official standing and into the No. 1 power position.

Atlanta lands second in the power ranking despite sitting fourth officially. The Dream beat Minnesota, received a record setting rebounding performance from Angel Reese and looked more dangerous at the end of the week than their place in the table alone suggests.

The teams running hotter than their records

RANKED HIGHERAtlanta, Dallas, Los Angeles and Seattle

Each sits two places higher in the We Know the W ranking than in the official standings.

RANKED LOWERMinnesota drops three

Back to back losses to Golden State and Atlanta create the largest gap on the board.

EXACT MATCHLas Vegas, Indiana, Washington and Connecticut

For these four teams, the power ranking and official standing tell the same story.

Dallas rises to sixth on the strength of a four game winning streak. Los Angeles moves to ninth after winning two straight and three of four. Seattle remains last in the official standings, but a win over Toronto and promising production from its young core lift the Storm to 13th in the power ranking.

Minnesota is still the team to catch

Dropping the Lynx to fourth is not a claim that three teams have built better seasons. Minnesota still owns the best record. Power rankings are allowed to hold two thoughts at once: the Lynx have been the league's best team across 40 games, and Golden State, Atlanta and Las Vegas carried better momentum into the break.

SHAK'S TAKEThe playoff field is locked. The playoff temperature is not.

The standings decide who earned the position. Power rankings help explain who opponents would least like to see right now. Same league, different measuring cup.
