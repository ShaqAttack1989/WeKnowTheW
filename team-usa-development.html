THE 5 ON 5 LADDER

AmeriCup first, World Cup next.

The junior calendar alternates by age group. U16 and U18 continental teams qualify the program for the following U17 and U19 global championships.

STEP 1U16 Women’s AmeriCup

The first major 5x5 national team stage for the youngest junior group.

STEP 2U17 Women’s World Cup

The qualified age group moves from the Americas to a global field.

STEP 3U18 Women’s AmeriCup

Older prospects face a continental championship with U19 qualification attached.

STEP 4U19 Women’s World Cup

The final junior 5x5 world stage before college stars move deeper into senior contention.

THE 3x3 DEVELOPMENT LANE

Halfcourt habits start early.

USA’s 3x3 pathway includes U18 World Cups, Nations League and U23 competition. The format develops fast decisions, physical finishing and defensive accountability in space.

U183x3 U18 World Cup

A first global halfcourt championship and an introduction to FIBA 3x3 eligibility.

U21 + U23Nations League

Multiple stops create repetitions, country points and a deeper player pool.

U233x3 U23 World Cup

A direct bridge from youth competition to the senior international game.

SENIORWomen’s Series + World Cup

The best prepared players can move into tour stops and senior medal assignments.

WHAT TO SCOUT

The box score is only the first page.

Junior dominance can hide the details. The real evaluation is how a player’s tools project when the size, speed and role demands rise.

DECISION MAKING

Can the advantage travel?

Watch how quickly a player reads help defense, moves the ball and adjusts when the first option disappears. Those habits transfer better than a scoring average alone.

ROLE RANGE

Can she help without being the star?

Junior teams collect primary scorers from across the country. The players who defend, screen, rebound and connect lineups show a senior ready layer.

PHYSICAL TRANSLATION

What changes against grown strength?

Length and athleticism matter, but balance, touch, footwork and contact tolerance often tell more about the jump to college and professional basketball.
