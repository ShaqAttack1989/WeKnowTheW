PREVIEW NOTES · AUGUST 30, 2026

What I am watching before USA tips off in Berlin

USA opens against China on September 4.

USA is the favorite. That part is easy. The better question is what this group looks like when twelve stars have to become one team in a short tournament where one rough night can change the path to a medal.

01THE OPENER

China makes USA prove it right away

The first game is a rematch of the 2022 World Cup final, and China brings the kind of size that can make even a loaded American frontcourt work for clean touches. USA should win, but this is not a soft opening night. It is the first real look at how quickly the defense, rebounding and pace are connected.

Watch the glass, second chance points and how quickly USA turns rebounds into offense.
02THE ROTATION

The hardest job belongs to Kara Lawson

Caitlin Clark and Paige Bueckers arrive for their first World Cup while Chelsea Gray, Kelsey Plum, Jackie Young and Rhyne Howard already know what it feels like to carry major possessions. Everybody can create. The test is whether the ball keeps moving when every player on the floor is used to being a first option somewhere else.

The best USA lineup may not be the five biggest names. It may be the five players whose games fit together fastest.
03THE STANDARD

A’ja Wilson still sets the temperature

Wilson was the 2022 World Cup MVP and the MVP of the Paris 2024 Olympics. She does not need this tournament to prove who she is. What matters is how her presence organizes everybody else. With Breanna Stewart, Napheesa Collier, Aliyah Boston and Angel Reese around her, USA can win with power, speed, length or skill.

If USA controls the paint without slowing the game down, the rest of the field has a serious problem.
04THE GROUP

First place is worth more than bragging rights

The winner of each group moves directly to the quarterfinals. Second and third place teams have to play an extra qualification game. That makes the three Group D games more than a warm up. USA has the depth to survive an extra game, but there is no reason to volunteer for one.

The clean path is simple: win Group D, rest, then make somebody else survive the extra round.
05GROUP D SWING GAME

China and Italy could decide the shape of the group

FIBA has circled China against Italy as the pivotal game behind USA. China has the interior size. Italy returns to the World Cup after a 32 year wait and arrives with confidence from its EuroBasket bronze. Czechia enters with injury concerns, which makes every result in the middle of the table matter even more.

USA should control the top of Group D. China and Italy may decide who gets the better road behind them.
06MEDAL RADAR

The real pressure is waiting outside Group D

FIBA's newest power ranking has USA first, France second, Australia third, Spain fourth and Belgium fifth. France came within one possession of taking Olympic gold in Paris. Australia has experience and size. Belgium can carve teams apart with passing. Spain has a path that could build confidence before the medal rounds.

USA is the favorite, but the tournament gets much more interesting once the bracket starts forcing styles to collide.
MY READ

USA should go 3 and 0 and win Group D. The title question is not talent. It is whether a team this loaded finds rhythm quickly enough that its depth becomes pressure instead of clutter. If that happens, everybody else is playing for the upset of the tournament.
