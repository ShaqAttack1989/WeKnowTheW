THE QUICK BITE

The crown has history on one side and pressure on both.

USA owns the streak. France owns the most explosive scoring profile of this tournament. Something has to give.

The United States walks into Sunday carrying 35 consecutive World Cup wins, four straight world titles and a 71 game winning streak across World Cup and Olympic play. The last World Cup loss came in 2006. Since then, the program has turned global pressure into routine, at least from the outside looking in.

France is the opposite kind of story. This is its first World Cup final and its first guaranteed medal since taking bronze at the inaugural tournament in 1953. Yet nothing about this run feels accidental. France is 5 and 0, averages 96.2 points and has outscored opponents by 35.6 points per game. USA is also 5 and 0, but at 87.6 points per game and a plus 27.8 average margin.

Then there is the memory neither team needs explained. The 2024 Olympic final ended USA 67, France 66. One point separated gold from heartbreak. Two years later, Gabby Williams and France get another shot at the same flag, this time for a trophy the country has never lifted.

WHAT DECIDES IT

Three questions, forty minutes.

The final is not really USA depth versus France shooting. It is whether either team can force the other to abandon what has made it comfortable.

01

Can USA make Gabby Williams work for every touch?

Williams enters the final as France's engine, scorer, connector and defensive disruptor. She had 22 points, five rebounds, five assists and four steals in the semifinal. USA has enough bodies to change the matchup possession by possession. The question is whether the changes slow her down or simply give her a new problem to solve.

02

Can France turn this into another one possession game?

Spain showed the route. Slow USA's early offense, make the half court crowded and keep the score close enough that every late decision becomes heavy. France has more offensive firepower than Spain and already knows what a one point championship game against USA feels like.

03

Which version of USA's depth arrives?

Breanna Stewart led the semifinal with 16 points. Jackie Young has been the team's leading scorer across the tournament, Caitlin Clark leads the assists, and Rhyne Howard has supplied the ball pressure. USA does not need one superhero. It needs its waves to stay connected.

THE PICK IS NOT THE POINT

USA should be favored because history, depth and late game defense have survived every test. France, however, has been the more explosive team by scoring margin. This is the rare final where calling the favorite obvious would ignore what the tournament has actually shown.
