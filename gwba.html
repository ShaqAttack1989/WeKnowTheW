A MIDWESTERN COURT OF ITS OWN

The women’s pro game has always had more than one home.

The Global Women’s Basketball Association brought independent women’s teams together for regional competition, with a circuit that included Flint, Wisconsin, St. Louis and Kansas City. It gave experienced players another place to play and gave local fans a closer look at high-level women’s basketball.

GWBA coverage sometimes used “professional” and “semi-professional” interchangeably. This archive uses independent league for the circuit and keeps the league’s record separate from teams’ titles in other organizations.

SEASONS THAT SHAPED THE STORY

From first tip to the last completed final

The GWBA timeline is a story of local teams building something year by year, with the title passing between two familiar powers.

2016

A new regional circuit tips off

The GWBA begins play. Flint wins the inaugural championship, and Arlesia Morse is named Finals MVP.

INAUGURAL SEASON
2017–18

Flint sets the early standard

The Monarchs win three consecutive GWBA championships, with Crystal Bradford was named Finals MVP during the run, and Flint closed 2018 with its third straight GWBA title.

FLINT · 3 IN A ROW
2019

Wisconsin GLO arrives and goes unbeaten

In its first GWBA season, GLO completes a 14–0 campaign. Taylor Wurtz’s late three helps seal an 80–78 championship win over Flint; Jessica Lindstrom is Finals MVP.

WISCONSIN · 14–0
2020

The pandemic pauses the league

The season is canceled during COVID-19. The circuit returns the following year.

SEASON CANCELED
2021

GLO holds on to the crown

Wisconsin defeats St. Louis 93–77. Kelly Moten scores 30 points and is named Finals MVP.

WISCONSIN · TITLE NO. 2
2022

Flint answers with a fourth GWBA title

The Monarchs edge Wisconsin 72–70 in a final decided in the closing seconds. It is Flint’s fourth GWBA championship.

FLINT · TITLE NO. 4
2023

Overtime closes the latest completed season

Wisconsin rallies to beat Flint 104–97 in overtime at Oshkosh Arena. The GLO earns its third league title in a four-season span.

WISCONSIN · TITLE NO. 3
2024

A transition, with no confirmed league ending

Wisconsin GLO announced a move to the IPWBL. In June, local reporting said the GWBA website was inactive and its social channels had not been updated. We found no official league notice declaring dissolution, so this archive records the 2023 final as the last completed season documented here rather than labeling the GWBA formally folded.

FRANCHISES ACROSS THE MAP

More than two names on a championship banner

Team lineups changed across seasons. These are selected GWBA franchises from the record, not a claim that every club played in every year.

FM

Flint Monarchs

The league’s first dynasty. Flint won the inaugural title and three in a row, then added a fourth GWBA crown in 2022.

GLO

Wisconsin GLO

Wisconsin’s first GWBA season was a perfect one. The GLO went on to win titles in 2019, 2021 and 2023.

SS

St. Louis Surge

A returning regional power that reached the 2021 championship game after an 11-game season, with just one loss.

KC

Kansas City Crossover

The 2023 expansion team gave the league another Midwest market and opened a new route for local fans to follow pro women’s basketball.

MA

Milwaukee Aces

One of the early clubs, and the opponent in Flint’s 2017 championship repeat.

AI

Akron Innovators

The inaugural final’s opponent as Flint claimed the GWBA’s first championship in 2016.

The league also featured clubs such as the Chicago Breeze, Indianapolis Bandits, Detroit Queens and others. Team names and participation varied by season.

WHY THIS LEAGUE BELONGS IN THE RECORD

Local basketball can carry a national story.

Independent leagues are often recorded through scattered box scores, local game reports and team releases. That does not make the history small. The GWBA’s championship trail connects Midwest communities, college standouts and players who kept competing after their campus careers.

01

More places to play

League owner Sonya Nichols described the aim as giving women more professional options beyond the WNBA. The 2023 KC Crossover launch reflected that regional purpose.

02

Local ties, real stakes

Wisconsin’s 2019 title team included Badgers and Green Bay standouts, and the GLO called its inaugural championship a chance to bring Wisconsin players together.

03

A record worth keeping

Seven completed championship seasons left a clear contest between Flint and Wisconsin. A searchable archive makes those results easier to find than a forgotten final score.
