SAN SALVADOR · NOVEMBER 1, 2026

Four wins carried USA to the final.

Pool B · first place2–0

USA beat Colombia 21–7 and Cayman Islands 21–4.

Quarterfinal · Paraguay21–18

USA held on to reach the semifinal.

Semifinal · Cuba21–17

USA won 21–17 and advanced to the gold-medal game.

Gold-medal final · Canada12–21 · SILVER

USA finished runner-up. Sydney Taylor was named to the Women’s Team of the Tournament.

Canada won its third women’s AmeriCup title. Michelle Plouffe earned tournament MVP honors; Colombia took bronze, its first AmeriCup medal.
