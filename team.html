THE W VAULT · FRANCHISE HISTORY

A franchise built to represent Atlanta.

The Dream arrived in 2008, found a contender quickly and continues to connect the league’s present to a city with deep basketball and cultural roots.

2008

The Dream begins

Atlanta entered the WNBA as an expansion franchise and became the league’s home in the Southeast.

2009

A cornerstone arrives

Atlanta selected Angel McCoughtry No. 1 overall. She won Rookie of the Year and became the centerpiece of the franchise’s first great era.

2010–13

Three trips to the Finals

The Dream won Eastern Conference titles in 2010, 2011 and 2013, reaching the WNBA Finals three times in four seasons.

2019–21

A new home and a new brand

The shooting-star identity was refreshed around Atlanta’s red, black, white and original Dream light blue as the team made Gateway Center Arena its primary home.

2021

New ownership

Larry Gottesdiener, Renee Montgomery and Suzanne Abair took ownership, beginning a new chapter centered on community, players and long-term growth.

2025–26

The Smesko era

Karl Smesko’s staff and Dan Padover’s front office shape the current contender around a core that includes Rhyne Howard, Allisha Gray and Brionna Jones.

THE LOCKER ROOM

Uniform eras and retired numbers.

Atlanta’s visual identity has moved from the original Dream light blue into a bolder city-led palette while keeping the shooting star at the center.

Original Dream blue

The franchise’s early light-blue identity remains part of the current star and symbolizes unity and loyalty.

Atlanta red, black and white

The 2019 rebrand made Atlanta’s energy and strength the foundation of the modern crest and uniform system.

Homegrown Rebel

The current black Rebel look uses peach accents to connect the team directly to Georgia and was designed with player input.

OWNERSHIP

Larry Gottesdiener, Renee Montgomery & Suzanne Abair

The investor group has led the franchise since 2021. Montgomery is also a former Dream guard and the first former WNBA player to become both an owner and executive of a WNBA team.
