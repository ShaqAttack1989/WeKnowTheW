THE SIX JOBS

Not every important role starts the game.

The Monday board is built around six repeatable archetypes so the picks can update without changing what the role means.

ROLESixth Woman

The first player off the bench who can change the rhythm without needing to start.

ROLEMicrowave Scorer

Can heat up in a hurry and turn a quiet stretch into a scoring run.

ROLE3 & D Wing

Spaces the floor, defends hard assignments and adds value without dominating the ball.

ROLEGlue Player

Screens, communicates, rotates, makes the extra pass and helps lineups make sense.

ROLEBackup Floor General

Keeps the offense organized and protects the team when the lead guard rests.

ROLEEnergy Big

Rebounding, rim pressure, screens, paint defense and effort possessions that flip momentum.
