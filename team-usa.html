THE HISTORY FILE

USA women’s wheelchair basketball, title by title.

The para game has its own national-team structure and governing bodies, but it belongs in the Team USA story. The world championship timeline shows how long the road back to gold really was.

1990The first women’s worlds

St. Etienne, France hosted the inaugural women’s World Championship. The United States won the first title.

2010World champions again

The U.S. captured its second women’s world championship, the title that stood as its most recent until Ottawa.

2016Rio Paralympic gold

Team USA reached the top of the Paralympic podium in Rio, its most recent Paralympic gold.

2024Paris silver

The U.S. women reached the Paralympic final and took silver, setting up another veteran-heavy championship cycle.

2026Back on top of the world

USA 77, Canada 76 in Ottawa. The third world title arrives sixteen years after the second.
