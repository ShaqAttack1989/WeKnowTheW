FORMAT MAP

Four design eras in one glance.

1999 TO 2005Team jerseys

All Stars represented their own franchises rather than wearing a separate event uniform.

2006 TO 2015East versus West

Conference colors, player numbers and team patches defined the event identity.

2018 TO 2019Captain selected teams

Black and white arrived with the new format, followed by host inspired Las Vegas colors.

2021 FORWARDFormat and host storytelling

Olympic matchups, captain teams and local palettes created more flexible uniform stories.
