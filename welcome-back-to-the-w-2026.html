WHY IT MATTERS

Seeding changes the road.

A playoff berth gets you into the room. Seed position changes which door you use. The last week can affect first round matchups, home court and which contender lands in your path.

1Check the seed, not just the record. Teams tied in wins can still have very different postseason paths.
2Watch the last ten and streak. They are imperfect, but useful clues for current rhythm.
3Watch workload. A returning international star may be asked to win now and recover later.

WATCH LAB

Four things to watch that are not points.

A watch guide should teach you how the game is moving, not just tell you who scored.

01

The first five minutes

Players returning from international basketball have changed teammates, terminology and roles. Watch communication, spacing and defensive matchups before legs become the obvious story.

02

The possession battle

Offensive rebounds and live ball turnovers create extra chances without requiring better shooting. Count who gets to shoot more often.

03

Paint touch, then what?

A paint touch is not only a layup attempt. It can collapse the defense, create a corner three or force a rotation that opens the second side.

04

The closing five

Do not assume the starters finish. The final lineup tells you what a coach trusts when the game becomes possession by possession.

FROM BERLIN BACK TO THE W

The jersey changed. The job might too.

International basketball can make a WNBA role look completely different. A primary creator abroad may return to a more balanced club offense. A switchable defender may have spent two weeks as a national team scorer. That role change is one of the best things to watch on return night.

LAS VEGASJackie Young

World Cup gold, then immediately back into an Aces team fighting for position. Watch whether her international scoring aggression travels home with her.

PORTLANDCarla Leite

France reached the World Cup final. Portland gets its young guard back for a late season stretch where decision making reps matter as much as the result.

ATLANTARhyne Howard

Atlanta already has playoff stakes. Returning from Team USA adds another layer: how quickly does a star move from national team role player back to club creator?
