FIRST, THE RULES

This is not a ranking of anybody's relationship.

Love is not a stat. Basketball is. So the tournament only asks one question: which publicly reported couple built the strongest basketball résumé while they were teammates?

That distinction matters. Paige Bueckers and Azzi Fudd have already asked people to keep the private part private while they handle the basketball part professionally. The same respect applies here. We are grading shared titles, shared peaks, fit, longevity and big stage impact. No points for matching outfits, anniversary captions or who remembers to take the trash out.

The field is WNBA connected rather than WNBA teammate only. Both players must have WNBA experience or a current WNBA connection, and the pair must have shared a real basketball roster somewhere. That keeps Gabby Williams and Marine Johannès eligible through France, and Breanna Stewart and Marta Xargay eligible through Dynamo Kursk, even though neither pair shared a WNBA roster.

FOOD FOR THOUGHT

The tournament says something bigger than who advances.

The W has always made room for players to be whole people. The basketball history is richer because some of those people built their careers beside the person they loved.

Diana Taurasi and Penny Taylor are the measuring stick because the shared basketball evidence is almost rude. Three Mercury championships, 2007, 2009 and 2014, with both players central to the same winning ecosystem. Taurasi supplied the pressure and shot creation. Taylor connected lineups, defended across roles and punished the space Taurasi created. Their Pair W Score does not win because they are an iconic couple. It wins because the same uniform produced three banners.

Courtney Vandersloot and Allie Quigley make the final because their fit may be the cleanest in the field. One bent the defense with passing, one bent it with shooting, and Chicago eventually turned that geometry into the 2021 championship. They were also the league's first married teammates, which makes the history important, but the bracket advances them because the basketball held up.

DeWanna Bonner and Alyssa Thomas are the pair that can break this model if Phoenix adds a championship chapter. They already have years of Connecticut contention, a 2022 Finals run, completely different offensive profiles and a shared appetite for difficult possessions. The Pair W Score rewards what is finished. Their story may not be.

Then come the challengers. Paige and Azzi own the most obvious ceiling because they already have the 2025 NCAA title, years of shared reps and now a Dallas chapter. Gabby and Marine might be the purest style pairing here, downhill force meeting improvisational shooting. Their France tape looks like two players who understand where the other wants the next pass before it leaves the hand.

That is the real Love & Basketball lesson. Chemistry is not magic. It is accumulated information. You learn where somebody wants the ball, what silence means after a bad possession, when to clear a side and when to cut behind the help. Most great teammates spend years learning that language. Some of these pairs happened to take it home with them.
