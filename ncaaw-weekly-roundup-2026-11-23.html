THE WEEK, IN CONTEXT

The early season is already asking real questions.

Across 43 games involving teams in the Nov. 16 AP Top 25, the ranked teams’ only two losses came against other ranked teams. No AP-ranked team fell to an unranked opponent. The week’s best closeout belonged to Ohio State; the biggest late-week statement belonged to Texas.

THE BIG STORYLINES

Six performances that shaped the week.

Every score and stat below links to an official school, conference or event recap.

01 · THE CLOSER

Cambridge gave Ohio State two 27-point nights.

Jaloni Cambridge scored 27 points and grabbed 12 rebounds as No. 19 Ohio State edged No. 19 Ole Miss, 60–57, on Nov. 18. The Rebels had a final three-point try to force overtime, but it missed. Four days later, Cambridge scored 27 again in Ohio State’s 75–61 loss to No. 6 Texas.

Ohio State recap and box score ↗
02 · TEXAS TAKES CONTROL

Cunningham reached 1,000 in a ranked win.

Breya Cunningham posted 24 points and 13 rebounds against Ohio State, reaching 1,000 career points. Texas opened the fourth quarter with a 16–0 run and finished 25-for-26 at the free-throw line.

Texas game recap ↗
03 · RECORD-TYING DEFENSE

Harpring made the WBCA Showcase a two-way clinic.

North Carolina freshman Kate Harpring scored 28 points and recorded five steals in a 61–47 win over Texas Tech. Her five steals tied the WBCA Showcase individual record.

WBCA official recap ↗
04 · THE 90-POINT TEST

UCLA stayed unbeaten through Tennessee’s best punch.

The No. 10 Bruins beat Tennessee 90–80. KK Bransford scored 25, Elina Aarnisalo added 22, and Kaylene Smikle answered with 27 points and 10 rebounds for the Lady Vols.

UCLA official recap ↗
05 · ALASKA, CLAIMED

Louisville left the Great Alaska Shootout with the trophy.

The No. 3 Cardinals beat Seattle 97–78 to win the tournament and move to 9–0. Carys Baker led Louisville with a season-high 24 points.

Louisville official recap ↗
06 · A BIG EAST STANDARD

Popovic powered another UConn conference opener.

Freshman Jovana Popovic scored 20 points on 8-for-11 shooting in an 83–43 win over Seton Hall. UConn extended its regular-season BIG EAST winning streak to 59 games.

UConn official recap ↗
