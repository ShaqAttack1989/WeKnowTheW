LATEST WOMEN’S 5x5 CHAMPION · 2025

USA reclaimed the crown.

The United States beat Brazil, 92–84, to win its fifth Women’s AmeriCup title. Mikayla Blakes was named tournament MVP, and the champions earned a place at the 2026 FIBA Women’s World Cup.
