CLOSED BRANCH

1997 to 2008

Houston Comets

The league's first champions became its first dynasty, winning the first four WNBA titles and setting a standard the rest of the league had to chase.

HOU
12seasons
241–149regular season
9playoff trips
4championships

Highlights left behind

  • Won four consecutive championships from 1997 through 2000.
  • Produced the league's first regular season MVP and first Finals MVP.
  • Finished with a .618 regular season winning percentage.

Names in the foundation

Cynthia Cooper, Sheryl Swoopes, Tina Thompson, Kim Perrot and coach Van Chancellor.
