WHY 3x3 PLAYERS HIT DIFFERENT

The skill set is exposed.

Possessions turn over instantly. Every player screens, handles, rebounds and defends in space. The best lineups combine strength, quick processing and the stamina to play without a quiet corner.

OFFENSE

Space, contact, quick reads.

Made baskets do not create a full reset. Teams clear the ball and attack again, which rewards players who can screen, slip, finish through contact and make the next read before the defense recovers.

  • Inside the arc counts one point.
  • Beyond the arc counts two.
  • The first team to 21 wins if it gets there before time expires.
DEFENSE

No weak link survives long.

With only three defenders, one lost assignment becomes an open rim or a two point shot. Switchability, rebounding and communication matter on every exchange.

  • One screen can force a mismatch.
  • Long rebounds start instant offense.
  • Fouls become increasingly expensive late.
THE OLYMPIC TRACK

Qualification has its own road.

Country ranking points, designated events and qualifying tournaments determine who reaches the Olympic field. A 5x5 résumé does not automatically create 3x3 eligibility.
