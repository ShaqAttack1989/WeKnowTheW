THE BENCH MOB

The game is bigger than the starters.

A home for role players, sixth players and specialists — plus a way to teach young fans why those jobs matter.

Sixth Player

The first player off the bench who can change the rhythm of a game without starting it.

Microwave Scorer

A player who can heat up quickly and create points in short bursts.

3-and-D Wing

Spacing on offense, difficult assignments on defense and value without needing the ball all game.

Glue Player

Screens, rotations, communication, extra passes and the little things that make lineups work.

Backup Floor General

Keeps the offense organized, manages pace and protects the team when the lead guard rests.

Energy Big

Rebounding, rim pressure, screens, paint defense and effort possessions that can flip momentum.

THE FILM ROOM

Learn to see what the players see.

Basketball education without assuming you already know the language. Positions are starting points — modern players often do several jobs at once.

Point Guard

Initiates offense, reads coverages, controls tempo and creates advantages for teammates.

Wing

Scores, cuts, spaces, defends across positions and often connects guards with bigs.

Post / Big

Protects the paint, rebounds, screens, finishes inside and can anchor offense from the high or low post.

Pick-and-Roll

A ball-handler uses a teammate’s screen to force defenders to choose between two threats.

Spacing

Good floor balance creates driving lanes, cleaner passing windows and harder defensive decisions.

Help Defense

Great defense is five people moving together — tagging cutters, protecting the rim and recovering to shooters.
