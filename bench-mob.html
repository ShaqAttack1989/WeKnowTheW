HOW THE JOBS ARE SCORED

Six different jobs. Six different stat profiles.

The board does not simply pick the six highest scorers. Each role has its own statistical recipe, and Shak’s Starting Five is removed from the Bench Mob pool.

ROLE 01Sixth Woman

Bench scoring, efficiency, PER, win-rate impact, playmaking and rebounding.

ROLE 02Microwave Scorer

Points per 36, usage, true shooting and 3-point production. Instant offense matters more than raw minutes.

ROLE 033 & D Wing

Real 3-point volume plus steals, blocks and defensive value from a wing-sized role player.

ROLE 04Glue Player · Floor General

Assist creation, assist-to-turnover control, ball security, steals and efficient connective play.

ROLE 05Backup Floor General

A true bench guard who can organize the second unit, create shots and keep turnovers under control.

ROLE 06Energy Big

Rebounds, offensive boards and blocks per 36, plus efficient interior impact in bench minutes.
