How the We Know the W Rating is calculated

The We Know the W Rating is a 0–100 editorial score built around three goals: a winning regular season, winning in the playoffs and winning a WNBA championship. Returning coaches earn up to 60 points from their WNBA career, 20 from 2026 results and 20 from titles.

  • Pre-2026 WNBA career regular-season win percentage × 30
  • 2026 regular-season win percentage × 10
  • Pre-2026 WNBA career playoff win percentage × 30
  • 2026 playoff win percentage × 10
  • 5 points per WNBA title as head coach, capped at 20

For a first-year WNBA head coach with no prior WNBA head-coaching record, the 2026 regular-season and playoff win rates each carry up to 40 points. Career components stop before 2026 so this season is not counted twice. Missing game samples earn zero for that component. Coach of the Year honors remain visible but do not affect the score. The total rounds to the nearest whole point. This is an editorial comparison, not an official WNBA statistic.
