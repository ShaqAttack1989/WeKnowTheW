NAME + LOGO + IDENTITY

Why Sirens works.

This isn't a Rockers reboot. The identity pulls from Lake Erie and the mythic idea of an unmistakable call — a visual and verbal language built around water, movement, sound and feminine power.

SIRENS
1
Lake Erie

Two blue tones connect the identity to Cleveland's lakefront; the lighter blue also nods to the old Rockers era.

2
Five in the flow

The Siren's flowing-hair details reference both the five Great Lakes and the five positions on a basketball team.

3
Sound waves

Curves and swashes around the figure are designed to feel like the call reverberating outward.

4
A brand with its own lane

Water and mythology give the team a world the Cavaliers do not already own.
